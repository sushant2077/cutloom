Privacy Policy

We built Cutloom to keep editing on your computer. This page explains what stays local, what leaves your device when you choose online features, and what the public website does.

Summary: Cutloom does not require a Cutloom cloud account or a paid AI API. Your local installation stores source media, transcripts, exports, social connection details and publishing records on your computer. If you use online imports or social posting, information is transmitted to the services you choose.

1. Who operates Cutloom

Cutloom is an independently maintained local-first creator tool published under the GitHub account sushant2077. For questions or support, use the project issue tracker. Never post passwords, tokens, private videos or sensitive personal information in a public issue.

2. Information stored on your device

Cutloom's local backend may retain videos you import, generated audio, transcripts, clip selections, render settings, exported MP4 files and publishing histories in its local application directory. When you connect external social accounts, credentials or OAuth tokens may also be stored in local application files. Protect your PC account and never upload those folders to public source-code repositories.

3. Local AI and optional network activity

Cutloom uses locally runnable components such as FFmpeg and whisper.cpp. Once downloaded, model inference can run on your computer without a paid API. Downloading software or speech models, importing videos using external links and checking for updates may contact third-party services. Optional tools and integrations can require additional network requests.

4. Social-media connections and publishing

If you explicitly authorize an external account and submit content, Cutloom sends the relevant video, title, caption, scheduling information and necessary authorization requests to the selected platform (such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok), according to the available integration. These platforms have their own privacy policies, permissions, upload limits and review processes. Not all connections support direct publishing.

5. This informational website

The public Cutloom website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not ask you to upload videos, sign in or enter social tokens. We have not embedded advertising, analytics trackers or forms. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical access information such as IP addresses according to its own policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

You control the local project and output files, and Cutloom offers local file-deletion controls. Deleting local media or tokens does not delete anything already posted to an external social platform. Use that platform's tools to remove published media or revoke its authorization.

7. Security

Cutloom's default server is intended for loopback access on your own computer. Do not expose it publicly or share saved account credentials. Avoid posting API keys, access tokens or credential files in GitHub repositories or screenshots.

8. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to connect social platforms, skip online imports, and run manual video editing without local AI. For privacy questions or to request changes to this policy, contact the project maintainer via the project repository without including confidential information.

9. Updates to this policy

This page may be updated as Cutloom changes. The effective date above identifies this version. We will update the policy when data handling or integrations materially change.
